ABOUT FEHB

A stronger community begins with an opportunity.

FEHB Organization is a community-focused organization working to help children, young people and families build the knowledge, confidence and practical skills for a more hopeful future.

We listen to local priorities and bring people together around useful learning, skills development and community initiatives. Our work is grounded in respect, participation and the belief that lasting progress begins locally.

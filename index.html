Who we are

A foundation built on dignity, opportunity and hope.

FEHB International is a non-political, non-religious and non-governmental foundation based in São Tomé and Príncipe.

We believe no vulnerable child or adult should be left without education or the chance to learn a trade. We work to help people gain knowledge, practical skills and the confidence to shape their own futures.

What we do

Practical support. Lasting possibilities.

Our work focuses on helping vulnerable people access learning, develop useful skills and participate fully in their communities.

▤

Education for all

School support for vulnerable children, including help with fees, books, uniforms, transport and feeding, alongside adult literacy and second-chance learning.

Learn more →
⚒

Vocational skills & livelihoods

Trade training, apprenticeships, starter tools and mentorship to help people build a livelihood and work with confidence.

Learn more →
♡

Disability inclusion & care

Promoting access, dignity and participation for persons with disabilities, and supporting vulnerable families through awareness and guidance.

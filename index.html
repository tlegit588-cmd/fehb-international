Who we are

A foundation built on dignity, opportunity and hope.

FEHB International is a non-profit, non-political, non-religious and non-governmental foundation based in São Tomé and Príncipe.

We champion 5 core pillars: Beauty, Agriculture, Education, Technology, and Humanitarian Development to empower vulnerable children, youth and adults.

✓ Love & dignity
✓ Inclusion & unity
✓ Honesty & discipline
✓ Hard work & service

Our Core Areas

The 5 Pillars of FEHB International

Our comprehensive approach covers beauty, agriculture, education, technology, and humanitarian development.

🌸

Beauty & Confidence

Professional grooming, cosmetics and vocational beauty training empowering individuals with self-esteem and independent trade skills.

🌱

Agriculture & Farming

Sustainable agriculture training, farming techniques, and local food security initiatives across São Tomé and Príncipe.

📚

Education & Literacy

School support, books, uniforms, tutoring, and adult literacy programs opening doors to lifelong learning.

💻

Technology & Digital Skills

Computer literacy, digital workshops, and technical training preparing youth for the modern digital economy.

🤝

Humanitarian Development

Comprehensive community care, disability inclusion, family support, and safeguarding vulnerable populations with unconditional compassion.

What we do

Practical support. Lasting possibilities.

Our work focuses on helping vulnerable people access learning, develop useful vocational skills, and participate fully in society.

Education for all

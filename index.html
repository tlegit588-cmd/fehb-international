Who we are

Local action. Lasting change.

FEHB Organization is a community-focused nonprofit working in São Tomé and Príncipe. We create practical opportunities for people to learn, grow and contribute to their communities.

Our approach combines education, vocational training, sustainable livelihoods, entrepreneurship and humanitarian support with the voices and needs of local people.
